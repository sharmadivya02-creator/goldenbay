What GoldenBay does with your data

Notice version — · written to be read, not to be scrolled past

Why we hold anything at all

How long we keep it

Emergency records — 30 days

Deleted automatically after that. A finished emergency is not useful to anyone a month later.

Health profiles and lab reports — until you delete them

These are yours. They stay until you remove them, and removal is immediate and total.

Live video from the CPR Co-Pilot — never stored

Frames travel to the hospital screen and disappear. Nothing is written to disk. The feed stops within eight seconds of the page closing.

What you can do

Get everything we hold

One tap downloads a file containing every record tied to a person — profile, documents, emergencies, lab reports.

Have it erased

One tap deletes it. Not marked as deleted — removed.

Withdraw consent

You can take back permission at any time without deleting the record.

Built — and honestly, not built

We are not claiming DPDP compliance. The items above are the parts of the Digital Personal Data Protection framework that a developer can build. Compliance is a legal status and needs more than code: a named grievance officer, breach notification procedures, and an assessment of the cross-border transfer that happens every time this app sends data to an AI model running on servers outside India. None of those exist here. Saying so is more useful to you than a badge would be.
